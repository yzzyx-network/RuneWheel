      How to use

      Main wheel

      Press SPIN to randomly pick an activity from the options currently on the wheel. The result is whatever lands under the pointer at the top.

      Sub-wheels

      Some activities have sub-options (e.g. Runecrafting → specific runes). After the main wheel lands on one of those, a smaller sub-wheel appears and spins automatically.

      • Click 🎡 on an activity to load its sub-wheel without spinning the main wheel.
      • Press SPIN SUB to spin the sub-wheel on its own.
      • Use ▸ on an activity to expand it and add or remove sub-options.

      Adding activities

      Type a name, pick a tag, and click Add. Options are saved in your browser (local storage).

      Tags & filters

      • Built-in tags: Boss, Skilling, Other, AFK.
      • Click + next to the tag dropdown to create a custom tag.
      • Click the ⚙ gear to manage or delete custom tags.
      • Filter buttons can be multi-selected (e.g. Boss + Skilling). All clears the filter.

      Show / hide activities

      • Click the 👁 eye on a row to hide that activity from the wheel (it stays in the list, greyed out).
      • Click the eye again to show it.
      • Enable all / Disable all applies only to activities in the current tag filter.

      Sorting

      The list is sorted by tag (Boss → Skilling → Other → AFK → custom), then alphabetically by name within each tag.

      Theme

      Use the switch in the top-right to toggle between the OSRS (stone & gold) theme and the Modern dark theme. Your choice is saved.

      Import & export

      Export downloads a JSON file with your activities, sub-options, custom tags, visibility, and theme. Import replaces your current data with a previously exported file (you will be asked to confirm).

      Clear all

      Removes every activity from the list. This cannot be undone (your tags remain).
